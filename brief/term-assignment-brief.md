## Term assignment

### Goal 1: Identify business problems, data and prospective solution

1. Select a real (existing) or imaginary company, make a research about its business strategies
2. By a System Thinking model, identify a problem statement: pain points (or opportunities) within the workflow of a selected business process or user journey map.
3. Based on the Balanced Score Card, identify the objective (strategy to solve the problem stated in (2)), target and the measure (expected outcome).
4. Make the exploratory data analysis (EDA) with a sample data set, sketch a system of data model, and their characteristics.

### Goal 2: Business Intelligent, Mining model, or Process Improvement

5. Components of the system solution (process redesign with UX/UI Choice, or Business Decision Improvement)
6. Show the data collection and treatment, with the interpretation to the relevant business.
7. Show the design of UX and UI for input, interaction, and output (if needed)
8. Show the intelligence or analysis process with the obtained results
9. Interpret the result of business process or decision improvement to the business strategy as planned.

### Checkpoints

- Goal 1: Written report in .PPT and presentation in Week 7 — **[50%]**
- Goal 2: Written report in .PPT and presentation in Week 15 — **[50%]**

### Deliverables

- Report: Written report in PPT slide via www.leb2.org a day before presentation day.
- **A sample customer journey map** — แนบภาพตัวอย่าง 1 ภาพ (ดู [`sample-customer-journey-map.png`](sample-customer-journey-map.png))
  เป็นตัวอย่าง "Customer Journey Map – Music Sharing Experience" (เคส Spotify) แสดงรูปแบบมาตรฐาน
  6 แถว: **STAGE → STEPS → THOUGHTS (คำพูดลูกค้า) → TOUCHPOINTS (ไอคอนช่องทาง) → ACTORS →
  EMOTIONS (กราฟอารมณ์ขึ้นลงพร้อมป้ายกำกับ)** — ใช้เป็นแม่แบบฟอร์แมตสำหรับ G1-B6
